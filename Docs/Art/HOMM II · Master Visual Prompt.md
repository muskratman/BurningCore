WORLD STYLE — Heroes of Might and Magic II (1996, New World Computing)

ART LANGUAGE
Hand-painted decorative fantasy panorama. Not pixel art as a style statement —
pixels are merely the delivery medium. True reference: late-medieval illuminated
manuscript meets fairy-tale book illustration meets theatrical castle diorama.
Semi-realistic: forms are recognizable and volumetric, but consciously exaggerated
for character and silhouette readability. Serious whimsy — not grimdark, not
children's cartoon.

COMPOSITION & SPACE
Wide horizontal cityscape view. Dense architecture — no empty zones.
Compressed, theatrical depth (not photographic perspective). Slight elevated
frontal-lateral angle, no cinematic lens distortion. Every tower and building
must read instantly as a distinct silhouette — architecture behaves like character.

LIGHTING
Soft, diffuse overcast-day or pre-dusk light — sky-lit, not spotlight-lit.
One weak directional accent is acceptable but must not break the decorative flatness.
No dramatic cinematic rim lighting. No harsh shadows from a single source.
Light feels ambient and enveloping.

LIGHT TEMPERATURE
Base: neutral-warm (approx. 5000–5500 K feel).
Magic elements, distant planes, and dark factions shift toward cool:
steel-blue, ash-grey, teal, cold violet.
The warm/cool contrast is the primary color drama of the image.

SHADOWS
Colored and soft — never pure black masses.
Shadow hues vary by faction:
  light factions → warm brown-grey, olive-brown
  dark/magic factions → blue-grey, violet-ash, cold purple
Shadow coverage: 27–51% of frame area. Deep but never opaque.

HIGHLIGHTS
Never blown-out white. Use: cream, bone, chalk, light sand, ivory.
Specular presence: ~5–15% of frame. Restrained, matte-ish sheen.

GAMMA / TONE
Midtones slightly pulled down — image reads as "twilight-saturated" rather
than bright or dark. Not washed out, not crushed. Dense tonal presence in
the mid-to-shadow range.

COLOR PALETTE (universal base)
Earthy browns, terracotta, ochre, dry sand, ash stone, olive & conifer greens,
steel blue, bone, ivory, tarnished copper, weathered slate.
Palette is always rich and disciplined — complex earthen-magical color mass,
never a two-tone scheme.

SATURATION
Medium to medium-high: approx. 0.30–0.59 range, mean ~0.41.
Saturated accents (magical glow, stained glass, foliage) never go neon.

TEXTURE & MATERIALS
Tactility is critical. Every surface must feel touchable:
rough hewn stone, dry fibrous wood, aged metal, moss, plaster, bone, slate tiles,
layered thatch. "Rich surface" matters more than physical accuracy.

MOOD SPECTRUM
  Warm factions (Knight, Sorceress) → noble feudal fairy tale, warm stone and wood
  Cold/dark factions (Warlock, Necromancer) → cold mineral fortress, gothic mausoleum,
  occult geometry — still decorative and composed, never chaotic or grimy

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No photorealism. No modern plastic 3D render. No grimdark dirty fantasy.
No empty compositions. No minimalism. No wide-angle distortion.
No hard cinematic back-lighting. No acid neon. No flat cartoon style.
No anime stylization. No deliberate chunky pixel showcase.
