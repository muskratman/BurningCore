# Mountain Biome Module

## Mountain Biome Vision

Горный биом должен создавать ощущение высокогорного фантастического ландшафта с крутыми обрывами, каменистыми тропами, обнажёнными скалами, холодным ветром, скудной растительностью и ощущением вертикального масштаба. Он должен выглядеть красиво, эффектно и привлекать к игре, а не казаться бесплодным или реалистичным.


Настроение может склоняться к:

- героическому
- загадочному
- спокойному
- холодному
- древнему
- волшебному

Типичные материалы горного биома:

- камень
- гранит
- сланец
- мох
- лишайник
- сухая трава
- карликовые альпийские деревья
- корни
- снежные пятна
- выветренное дерево
- горный туман


## Notes for the Mountain Biome

Рекомендуемая визуальная атмосфера:

- airy, cold, elevated
- scenic but playable
- rocky and natural rather than snowy unless the level specifically uses snow
- dwarf alpine trees for scale
- moss and lichen for surface richness
- narrow paths and vertical cliffs for platforming structure

Рекомендуемые исключения, характерные для биома:

- tropical vegetation
- flat terrain
- dense forest canopy
- modern roads or buildings
- overly smooth cliffs
- excessive snow unless the mountain level is winter-based

## Mountain Foreground Prompt

```text
Design the foreground layer for a 2.5D side-scrolling fantasy scene set in a high mountain biome. Use large near-camera rocks, cliff edges, broken stone slabs, exposed roots, tufts of wind-swept grass, dwarf alpine shrubs, and occasional weathered wooden elements.

The foreground should emphasize elevation, depth, and rocky natural forms while framing the playable space. Use painterly hand-painted 3D surfaces with a bright fairytale fantasy feel inspired by Heroes of Might and Magic II. Keep the silhouette clean, readable, and strong from a side-view camera.

Add subtle signs of altitude such as moss on stone, lichen, dry grass, and cold mountain air. The scene should feel layered and dramatic, but still supportive of gameplay readability.
```

## Mountain Midground Prompt

```text
Design the midground layer for a 2.5D side-scrolling fantasy scene set in a high mountain biome. This is the main traversal area and should contain rocky platforms, narrow paths, ledges, steps carved into stone, suspended bridges, scattered boulders, and small alpine trees.

The terrain should feel playable, believable, and vertically interesting, with a strong sense of mountain scale. Use stylized hand-painted 3D surfaces, natural stone variation, and fairytale fantasy color harmony. The scene should feel like a handcrafted mountain level from a storybook fantasy platformer.

Include environmental details such as moss, lichen, wind-shaped grass, and distant cliff layers. Keep the composition organized and readable, with a clear route through the level.
```

## Mountain Background Prompt

```text
Design the background layer for a 2.5D side-scrolling fantasy scene set in a high mountain biome. Show distant mountain peaks, layered cliffs, mist-filled valleys, faraway rocky ridges, and atmospheric sky space.

Keep the background elegant, simplified, and scenic. The forms should be large and readable, with soft atmospheric perspective and subtle color variation. The background should reinforce altitude, distance, and world scale without competing with the playable area.

The overall look should feel like a painted fantasy landscape with a calm, cold mountain atmosphere and a bright storybook fantasy sensibility.
```

## Mountain Texture Prompt

```text
Create a seamless tileable PBR texture for a mountain biome material in a bright fairytale fantasy world. The texture should be suitable for hand-painted 3D assets in a 2.5D side-scrolling platformer.

Possible mountain materials include granite, slate, weathered rock, mossy stone, lichen-covered stone, dry grass, alpine bark, and aged wood.

Generate a clean, production-ready material with stylized but physically believable surface detail. Use neutral lighting, no strong directional shadows, no perspective distortion, and no embedded objects unless they are part of the actual material. The surface must tile perfectly and remain visually consistent across a game environment.

The texture should match the Heroes of Might and Magic II inspired art direction: elegant, painterly, rich in color harmony, and handcrafted. If supported, include albedo, normal, roughness, ambient occlusion, and height information.
```
